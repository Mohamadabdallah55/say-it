# Football answer media standard

Applies to every football category and every difficulty (200, 400, 600).

- Player answer: real photograph of the exact player in the relevant club or national team kit. Do not substitute a different player or unrelated club kit.
- Shirt number: photograph of that player with the requested number visible, preferably in the specified season. Do not infer a shirt number from another player with the same number.
- Match result or date/season of a specific match: authentic final-whistle, scoreboard or post-match celebration photograph from that exact fixture; prefer the actual full-time scoreboard for a score question. No invented scoreboard or photo from a different fixture.
- Coach or trophy answers: relevant person and period or exact winning team's celebration.
- Answer illustrations must be hidden until reveal on both host and projector. Existing photo-identification questions may keep their clue image before reveal.
- Use imageUrl and imageSourceUrl (or the bank's corresponding afterImageUrl/credited media fields). Preserve credit/license metadata where applicable; source attribution does not itself establish reuse rights. Prefer rights-cleared assets, official source links and appropriately sized media. Do not add fake or unverified images, generic club logos as substitutes, or unreviewed videos.
- Record missing media as a review item, not as a completed question-media audit. Verify that images actually load on the published game and that no answers leak before reveal. Avoid preloading the entire bank.
