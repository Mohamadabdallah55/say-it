# Career entry years
Each existing selected club stop has an entry year, aligned by index with the club array. The display uses senior-team entry or debut years where appropriate, not childhood academy enrollment; reserve entry is explicitly marked. Loan and return labels distinguish some repeated stops. A club whose identity is the answer remains masked; its year is a clue. Recent transfers and selected ambiguous stops were checked against official club/competition reports and Reuters; remaining historical dates are editorial estimates pending individual source checks. These are selected stops, not a comprehensive transfer register.
