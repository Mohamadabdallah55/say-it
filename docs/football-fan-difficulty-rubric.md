# Football fan difficulty rubric

User-confirmed World Cup anchors: final assist, famous knockout upset and winning coach are 200. Official ball name and famous stadium name are 400. Obscure historical details and specific penalty takers are 600. Calibrate against an active football fan, and for club categories against a supporter of that club. For visual categories difficulty depends on recognition of the actual asset; for ordering it depends on the particular sequence, not card count alone. Do not force equal quotas or inflate well-known facts to fill hard tiers.

This pass preserves IDs, questions, answer texts and existing media, and adjusts selected mismatched tiers. It does not independently verify every factual answer, every shirt-number photo or external image licence. Some categories need additional genuinely difficult new questions and better match/venue/season imagery, rather than assigning easy items higher values.

## Updated audience standard (2026-11-26)
- 200: a casual viewer or non-specialist can answer; iconic names, basic rules, famous clubs and highly memorable events. A clue can make a recognizable answer accessible.
- 400: regular fans; mix approachable questions with moderately demanding details. Do not make the whole tier obscure.
- 600: an unusually attentive fan should have a fair chance; precise roles, substitutions, opponents, historical context, lesser-known records and tactical or transfer details, with unambiguous clues and reliable sources. Avoid artificially obscure trivia.
- Diversify across nearby questions and across score levels; goals, assists, braces and penalties are related families, not independent variety.
- Global football and football letters are text-first; no new images required.

## Casual tier correction
200 must be solvable by a casual viewer; add recognizable context (nationality, iconic club, famous nickname or first name where needed), not exact dates, old lineups, or niche statistics. For 600 favor memorable modern matches and substitutions, tactical details, or meaningful statistics over obscure pre-1990 trivia. Reuse already bundled player portraits where identity is exact; keep jersey and match-specific photos separate. Existing archived local images are reused rather than importing new Wikimedia images.
