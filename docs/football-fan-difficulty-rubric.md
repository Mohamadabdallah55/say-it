# Football fan difficulty rubric

User-confirmed World Cup anchors: final assist, famous knockout upset and winning coach are 200. Official ball name and famous stadium name are 400. Obscure historical details and specific penalty takers are 600. Calibrate against an active football fan, and for club categories against a supporter of that club. For visual categories difficulty depends on recognition of the actual asset; for ordering it depends on the particular sequence, not card count alone. Do not force equal quotas or inflate well-known facts to fill hard tiers.

This pass preserves IDs, questions, answer texts and existing media, and adjusts selected mismatched tiers. It does not independently verify every factual answer, every shirt-number photo or external image licence. Some categories need additional genuinely difficult new questions and better match/venue/season imagery, rather than assigning easy items higher values.
