# خمن الدولة المظللة — خرائط العالم

100 silent map questions: 30 easy, 30 medium, 40 hard. Version 2 uses full-continent framing (768×720 PNG), pale blue water, cream-colored land, thin gray boundaries, and dark red target country. No text, place names, flags, or labels appear inside images. Every question keeps its stable ID; new image URLs use images-v2/ to avoid old cached images.

Source: [D3 Graph Gallery world.geojson](https://github.com/holtzy/D3-graph-gallery/blob/master/DATA/world.geojson), simplified Natural Earth geographic boundaries. These are educational illustrations; small islands, lake details and disputed borders are generalized. Renderer: scripts/countryMapRasterizer.js.

The bank is listed under the official World and History category (menuGroup: world) in decks/index.json.
